## Prompt usati

### study-desk.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative website hero illustration
for Aula, a student-built learning platform. Create one original refined but
charming editorial illustration on a genuinely transparent background, no UI,
no lettering, no labels, no typography. Wide landscape composition, about 3:2,
all objects fully inside the frame with comfortable transparent padding.
A cosy informal study still life: a slightly open cream notebook, dark
forest-green over-ear headphones resting by it, one small ceramic terracotta
coffee mug, and a modest little potted leafy plant. An original small
folded-paper creature sits casually on the notebook with tiny dot eyes,
minimal understated face, thin dark green arms and legs and simple terracotta
shoes; friendly young-adult indie-bookshop character, not a child, not any
famous mascot. Keep character a pleasant secondary presence, the notebook
and headphones convey the main scene. Warm off-white paper, muted forest
green, terracotta and small butter-yellow accents. Elegant playful hand-drawn
ink linework and flat softly textured colors, restrained paper grain only
INSIDE the drawn objects, cutout silhouette with clean transparent edges.
Objects compose one unified appealing vignette, sophisticated
independent-magazine illustration. No background rectangle, no colored
backdrop, no detached shadows extending to the image boundaries. No
mathematical graphs, no equations, no computers, no graduation caps, no cartoon
text bubbles, no photorealism, no plastic 3D. This art must work beside elegant
Georgia-style serif headings on a warm ivory website.

### paper-companion.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small original decorative mascot
cutout for an editorial student learning website called Aula. ONE simple
folded cream paper character on a genuinely transparent background, no
lettering, no text, no UI, no background shapes. It has two small dot eyes,
minimal friendly face, thin forest-green arms and legs, and small muted
terracotta canvas shoes. The character is sitting in a relaxed pose on a small
closed forest-green book, with one hand raised in a modest hello. Full
character and book within image with generous transparent padding. Charming
tasteful young-adult indie-bookshop editorial illustration with hand drawn
dark green ink outlines, flat cream / forest-green / terracotta colors, very
subtle grain INSIDE the objects. It should feel like a quietly friendly brand
detail, not a noisy baby cartoon. No sparkles, no trophies, no graduation cap,
no famous character likeness, no surrounding scene, no solid background, no
drop shadow touching frame boundaries. Square composition.
